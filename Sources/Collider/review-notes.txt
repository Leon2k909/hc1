Collider - reviewed native-v2 build, 2026-10-15

The saved map follows the blue accelerator laboratory reference: a two-level
research hall, glazed accelerator chamber, control rooms and red access doors.
The current geometry adds open metal stairs, bevelled railings and cases,
service-room windows, suspended cable trays, pipes, flanges and valves.

All 68 authored positions passed native floor support, player-box clearance and
complete navigation paths. All four stair routes passed, including clearance
beneath their high ends. The current local TDM check selected the player's
camera and observed eight of nine bots moving during its four-second sample.

A separate game process joined the host. Both processes loaded the same four
Collider models and materials, two distinct player pawns with correct ownership
and their local player cameras. A controlled host-pawn teleport of about 100
units appeared in both before/after snapshots. This validates position
replication; it is not a keyboard traversal or Internet latency test.
The owned client was closed and all temporary test settings restored.

The saved lighting uses the accepted v4 two-pass indirect bake (2205 probes)
and four v3 static reflection cubes. Eleven practical lights have wider floor
coverage. Eight native editor views plus live host/client captures are under
output/collider/captures; the map-picker image matches the current art view.
The rejected v3 indirect bake remains historical and must not be adopted.
Use serial probe relocation and the ordinary camera/sky-compiler reflection
workflow; the default parallel relocation and GPU GGX bake are unstable here.

Short editor-hosted performance samples averaged 12.85 ms (77.8 FPS) and
14.05 ms (71.2 FPS in the lower hall). The raw report retains startup/GC hitches
and the missed 90 FPS target. These samples are not a full-match benchmark.

Build with: rtk python Tools/collider_package_native.py
output/collider/current-build.json points to the current archive. The packager
checks saved/staged asset identity, evidence freshness, seven lighting textures,
archive CRC and every packaged file's SHA256. Older detail-v2-review, native-v1
and offline-v2 archives are historical; their pending-review notes are obsolete.

This is an overlay for the existing hc1 repository, relying on its shared
materials, prefabs, helpers and gameplay code. GameUtils.cs already registers
Collider and is intentionally excluded. When the editor is open, use the
native import scripts; never overwrite an open scene from the filesystem.
