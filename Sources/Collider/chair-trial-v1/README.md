# Collider operator chair: accepted

Prepared and accepted 2026-10-29 after the user accepted the wall monitor and requested another improvement. The user selected this chair with “yeah after is way better”. Its exact reviewed render geometry is now installed in Collider's source assets. No editor launch, connection or scene interaction is used.

The baseline is the chair at plan `(1723,-55,256)` next to the east control desk. All 100 render triangles, UVs and normals are copied directly from the saved `collider_structure.obj`, after matching the existing generator's chair subset exactly. Its rectangular seat, rigid back, crossed bars and simple pedestal are the actual map geometry.

The proposal reuses and isolates the project's unfinished `collider_operators.chair` draft. It adds shaped upholstery with seat compression and a rolled front edge, a separate back shell and support spine, adjustable armrests, tilt tension/height controls, a gas lift, five cast spokes, swivel forks and twin caster wheels. It has a slimmer ergonomic profile than the original block chair. The material set includes cloth weave color/GL-normal/roughness, subtly textured molded plastic, painted metal and brushed alloy. Only the four used materials and their twelve textures are copied into the candidate's private material directory; the larger station draft is unchanged.

Accepted resources: `Assets/models/props/collider/chair-trial-v1/after.{obj,mtl,vmdl}`. Materials: `Assets/materials/collider/chair-trial-v1/`. The 100 old render triangles were replaced with the reviewed 8,672 at the same origin. The unused standalone `before` resources were removed from Assets and retained for rollback under `output/collider-chair-accepted-20261029/retired-before-resources/`. The original 44 x 46 x 52 inch player clip is unchanged. The slimmer visual shape and collision fit should be reviewed in the engine before native acceptance; no movement or performance result is claimed.

`candidate.json` records original review provenance and hashes. `installed.json` records the three changed structure source files and their before/after hashes. The structure now has 223,235 triangles. All unrelated original face lines and attribute tables, existing material bindings, the full physics suffix and the saved scene are preserved. Detail sources containing the accepted case/cabinet are byte identical; the accepted monitor is retained. Four private material bindings are added. Matching primary staged sources are synchronized without replacing a newer stage.

`Tools/collider_chair.py` and the selected `collider_map.control_desk` branch preserve regeneration. The original station helper still emits the existing desk and player clip; only this chair's visual geometry is substituted. `verify_accepted.py` checks exact approved positions, UVs, normals and material remaps, whole-station geometry parity, identical station collision, unrelated station parity and source scope. `verify.py` retains checks for finite attributes, winding, degeneracy, fabric UV area, material/texture references and identical standalone collision bytes. Evidence is in `output/collider-chair-accepted-20261029/`.

The private proposal corrects the draft's XY-only seat UVs, which collapsed at the vertical cushion edge and pinched the fabric normal map. Per-face physical projections keep the weave's scale on cushion shoulders as well as broad faces.

`prepare_render.py` creates the established actual-OBJ Blender renderer with matched full-chair camera settings. Images and evidence are in `output/asset-improvement-20261029/chair/`. These are offline renders with identical scale, lighting, samples and exposure. Native material/model compilation, appearance, collision fit, LOD and performance validation remain pending.
