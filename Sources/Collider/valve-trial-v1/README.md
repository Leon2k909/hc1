# Collider shutoff valve: accepted surface revision

Prepared and accepted 2026-10-29 after the user accepted the operator chair and requested another model. The user selected surface revision 2 with “yeah very good replace old with new, delete old”. Both existing valve instances in Collider now use the exact reviewed revision in the source mesh. Work remains offline: no s&box launch, connection or scene interaction.

The baseline is the existing overhead shutoff valve centered at plan `(-1280,280,770)`, with its handwheel facing east. `audit.py` matches all 678 installed triangles in `collider_detail.obj`; `build.py` copies their actual positions, UVs and normals. The original consists of a basic pipe body, low-sided flange rings, a square-spoke handwheel and a straight stem. This is saved map geometry, rather than an uninstalled generator draft.

The proposal adds a shaped cast body and separate bonnet/packing collar, gasket seams, stepped flange faces, eight flange fasteners per side with washers/nuts, an exposed helical stem thread, a rounded handwheel with curved spokes and a retaining nut, plus a small identification plate. The handwheel has its own analytic torus normals and explicit UV unwrap.

Surface revision 2 follows the user's feedback that the first proposal looked too smooth. The original proposal, all source materials and its exact render are preserved under `output/asset-improvement-20261029/valve/revisions/smooth-v1/`. The casting now has small geometric waviness and a restrained parting trace, with matching geometric normals. Distinct unwrapped casting/bonnet/flange/stem surfaces provide casting pits, orange peel, larger-scale fading, upper-face dust, shoulder paint loss, bolt-centered grime/oxidation streaks and grease near the packing. The grip has chipped paint/primer edges and exposed-metal response, with finer coating texture than the rough housing. Machined hardware has directional scratches, tarnish and uneven polish; hex fasteners have varied rotation. The bored throat has separate cylindrical UVs and a smooth machined finish. Twelve source materials include albedo, GL-normal, roughness and metalness maps. The private renderer reads metalness maps using the same material inputs as the native `complex.shader` resources.

Accepted resources: `Assets/models/props/collider/valve-trial-v1/after.{obj,mtl,vmdl}` and `Assets/materials/collider/valve-trial-v1/`. Baseline / accepted mesh: 678 / 20,164 triangles per instance. Neither authors collision. The two old instances at plan `(-1280,280,770)` and `(1280,280,770)` were replaced: 1,356 old render triangles removed and 40,328 approved triangles inserted. The packed detail mesh now has 227,608 triangles. The unused standalone `before` game resources are deleted; rollback copies remain outside Assets under `output/collider-valve-accepted-20261029/retired-before-resources/`. No native performance or LOD acceptance is claimed.

`candidate.json` retains the original review fingerprints. `installed.json` records the three changed detail model sources, all approved material hashes and the two placements. All other packed sources, saved scene, full detail physics suffix, unrelated original face lines/attribute tables and existing material bindings are preserved. Twelve private material remaps are added. Matching primary staged sources are synchronized, with newer stages guarded. Previous case/cabinet detail hashes are historical; this receipt supersedes them while the scope checks prove those meshes are retained. Monitor/chair structure sources are byte identical.

`Tools/collider_valve.py`, `collider_services.valve` and `collider_map.configure` retain the approved mesh, transforms, UVs, normals and materials on regeneration. `_legacy_valve` remains an offline verification reference; it no longer generates either active valve. The two legacy assemblies were matched independently because the old flange direction was not mirrored consistently. `verify_accepted.py` checks zero old valve triangles, exact reviewed attributes in both instances, retained case/cabinet geometry, generator parity, original physics, existing remaps and all unrelated source hashes. `verify.py` checks nondegenerate finite geometry/UVs/normals, winding, dependencies, no new physics and the wheel/bore details.

`prepare_render.py` fits one common camera to both meshes with a margin around their complete silhouettes. The established actual-OBJ Cycles renderer uses the same lights, lens, scale, sample count and exposure for both. The revised model is also compared directly with the archived smooth proposal at identical settings. Images, camera/framing data, source audit and verification evidence are in `output/asset-improvement-20261029/valve/`. These are offline renders, not native screenshots. Engine compilation, in-game appearance/placement, LOD and performance checks remain pending.
