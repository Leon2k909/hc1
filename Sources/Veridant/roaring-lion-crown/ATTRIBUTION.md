# Lion Statue — beachmouse

"Lion Statue" by beachmouse, a photogrammetry scan of a cast lion statue outside a library in
Burlingame, California.

Licence and author verified against the Sketchfab public API on 2026-11-08 (`source-metadata.json`).
Downloaded from the public Objaverse mirror (`objaverse-path.json`) as `beachmouse-lion.glb`.

Adaptation for HC1: the scanned slab is cut away, the sculpture is rescaled, re-centred on its open
mouth, mirrored into an opposed pair, decimated into three LODs, unwrapped and given a baked verdigris
bronze finish (new colour, normal, roughness, metalness and occlusion maps). The pier and the bronze
base are original to HC1. Include this attribution with distributions of the adapted model or the game.
