# Sculpted head and mane candidate

Editable source is `Assets/models/props/shared/veridant_fountain_reference_top_v4/heads.blend`.
Build with Blender 5.1 using `Tools/veridant_sculpt_heads_v4.py` in background mode.

The head has a remeshed continuous skull, brow, muzzle and jaw, boolean eye
sockets, nostrils, ear recesses and oral cavity. Separate inset eyes, canines
and tapered swept mane locks are modeled geometry. All replacement surfaces
have packed UV islands, independent of the old side-projected source image.
The current material is procedural bronze; a bespoke painted UV texture set
has not been authored. Original lower bodies and stone architecture remain.

The complete candidate is 468,969 triangles after export packing. The original
v2 and trial v3 models are retained. Native import timed out and the editor
became unresponsive. The candidate has NOT passed live viewport review, and
After an authorized editor restart, the compacted asset compiled and was
installed temporarily for live viewport review. Review FAILED: stylized face,
overly regular narrow mane locks, and an open junction to the retained torso.
The scene was restored to v2 with mouth coordinates 26.4,0.5,68.9 and saved.
This remains a rejected modeling candidate, not a validated reference match.
