The sculpture was scanned at the British Embassy in Cairo by Daniel Pett.
Adaptation removes scanned masonry and ground, fits two copies to Veridant's
upper basin, retains source UVs, and applies a procedural cast bronze material.
Central pier and plinth geometry is locally authored. This asset is a different
historical lion sculpture, not a reproduction of the generated prototype.
