# Seated Lion — Rigsters

“Seated Lion” by Rigsters, a scan of the sculpture by Antoine-Louis Barye /
Alexis Rudier Fondeur (Paris), 1848, at Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek, Copenhagen.

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/seated-lion-e7e957b26c4b480ab2daf47fe93beede

Source metadata and licence verified 2026-11-08 and retained in
barye-metadata.json. Public Objaverse distribution path is recorded in
objaverse-paths.json. Source geometry, UVs, colour texture and normal map are
retained. Adaptation selects the textured sculpture from the original comparison
scene, rotates, scales and duplicates it for the fountain, and tints the material
to aged bronze. New plinth and limestone pier are locally authored.
