# Riverside Lucky Stop storefront depth — native review

Next building-realism proposal after the user approved the Orion undercroft. Both storefront windows previously used a luminous shelf-picture card over an uncut six-inch wall. Existing-upgrade audit found no completed shop-window replacement. T2's accepted sliding-window construction is reused at proper 90×52 and 70×52 shop dimensions, keeping hardware at its original physical size. The accepted template's pure model function is extracted without executing or altering its historical builder. Existing finishes, actual stocked shelves, counter/register, fridges, ice cabinet, sign, steps, lights and other imported models remain unchanged.

The private current store base removes exactly 20 original faces: eight from both sides of the front wall panels, and twelve flat picture/backing/neon faces. The outer and inner wall faces are subdivided round two real holes, with original UV projections preserved and actual jamb/head/sill returns. New offset slider frames, seals, latch, tracks/weep slots, formed sill and glass occupy the openings. The glass now looks into the existing modeled shop. No new room or lights are fabricated. Original neon artwork is retained behind the glazing on thin backing plates and hangers; the beer plate is positioned within the right sash so the centre stile does not cross its lettering. No new image/material assets are created.

Candidate LODs are 5,440 / 4,208 / 2,000 triangles, at 0/25/70 thresholds. Complete public store ModelDoc, all seven original store LOD groups/imports and full physics are unchanged in the private preview apart from its base-source path. No new collider or gameplay change; the original wall collision remains. Original public assets and the active Riverside editor scene remain unchanged until approval.

`build.py` exact-matches all 20 saved faces and stages private resources outside Assets. `review.py` imports/compiles through the native editor, then renders matching before/after context and oblique detail views in a disposable scene using existing map lighting, no inspection fill or play/scene switch. The store and candidate are forced to LOD 0 for the matched comparison. `verify.py` checks protected hashes, exact retained records, ModelDoc/physics/import preservation, finite nondegenerate geometry/winding for all three LODs, correct remaining wall area on both planes, glazing setback and native state/model receipts. Evidence: `output/riverside/storefront-depth-v1/native` and `verification.json`. No gameplay, LOD-transition or frame-time benchmark claimed.
