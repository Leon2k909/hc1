## Firing range bench v2 (`models/gunsmith_range/shooting_bench_v2`, `Sources/GunsmithRange/bench-v2/`, 2026-10-31)
- Replaced the firing range's four benches at the user's word after an in-engine before/after ("Install it now"). The old one was a flat top on four square posts, and the player spawns right behind one.
- `Tools/gunsmith_range.py`: `bench_collision` writes the bench's boxes with no faces, and `scene()` places "<distance> m Bench" at (-19, lane y, 0), yaw 0, with no collider. Its axes: length along Y, the shooter on -X, +X downrange.
- **New collision** beside the old top and four legs: the two centre legs, the aprons, the stretcher, the shelf and ammo cans (metal), and the two canvas rests (fabric). The diagonals, low rails, spare bag, tray and brass have none.
- Installed with `install_range.py`: scratch run, comparison object by object, then copying only what changed. An unchanged run matched every live model file and scene object first, so a rerun of the tool is safe. The old faces cut were 896 of 3,848 triangles.
- About 26k / 6k / 1k triangles (LOD thresholds 30, 80). One material, one 4096 x 2048 atlas, about 18 px/in on the top. Painted from the old bench's oak photos in `Sources/GunsmithRange/bench/`. `share()` in `bench.py` sets each island's share of the atlas.
- The engine showed faults Blender would have hidden: edge wear running as pale dashes along long tubes, stave gaps reading as decking, and regular stripes on the rests. All fixed before the install.
- `bench_oak`, `bench_oak_endgrain` and `B.tabletop` in `Tools/gunsmith_range_bench.py` are no longer drawn. They're left in place, so going back is the old `level()` and `scene()` from git plus a rerun of the tool.
- Checked in an isolated editor scene with the player camera's post stack, including rays at each lane's top, rest, centre leg, cans and shelf (`capture_installed.cs.txt`). **Not yet seen in a match or with a gun in hand.** Shots are in `output/asset-improvement-20261030/range-bench/`.
