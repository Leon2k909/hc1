## Cerro Blanco's extinguishers (`models/props/extinguisher-v2`, all 7, 2026-10-31)
- The user saw the map's own extinguisher, a plain red cylinder with a stub on top, and said to replace it. Every one is now Railhead's `models/props/extinguisher-v2/extinguisher.vmdl`, with its own bracket and sign. Riverdale's had the same swap: see note 182.
- `Sources/CerroBlanco/extinguisher-v2/install_extinguishers.py` rebuilt each old one from the fit-out's numbers and cut 96 triangles from `cerroblanco_fitout_fine.obj`, matching every corner, 672 in all. No full map run. The scene's "Extinguishers" group under "Cerro Blanco" places 7 renderers. The old ones had no collision, so neither do these. Originals are in `output/asset-improvement-20261030/cerroblanco-extinguisher/before-install/`.
- `cerroblanco_fitout.door_fittings` now only records a placement in `Tools/cerroblanco_extinguishers.py`, and `cerroblanco_map.write_scene` adds the group. `verify_extinguishers.py --build` builds the map in memory and compares.
- The new model is wider. Clearance was measured against everything the scene's models draw: three ground-floor ones would have touched a painting's frame, so `BESIDE_ART` stands them 2 in along the wall.
- The old bodies were inside out: `cerroblanco_parts.rod` winds its faces against the normals it stores. The swap removes those 7, but the helper still draws the map's other round bars that way, and that's not fixed here.
- The model has no LODs: 7,430 triangles each, about 52,000 for all 7 where the old ones were 672.
- **Not compiled or seen in s&box yet: the editor was closed.** `cerroblanco_fitout_fine.vmdl_c` and `cerroblanco.scene_c` are older than their sources, so the game draws the old ones until they recompile.
