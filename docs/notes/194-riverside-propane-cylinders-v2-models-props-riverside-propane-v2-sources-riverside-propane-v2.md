## Riverside's propane cylinders v2 (`models/props/riverside/propane_v2`, `Sources/Riverside/propane-v2/`, 2026-11-03)
- Replaced all six at the user's word after a before/after ("Yes, install them"): the pad's row of three 100 lb cylinders, the 100 lb one on a terrace trailer's porch, and the 20 lb pair on the camper's tongue.
- The old ones were plain tubes with a cone and a black cap. The new ones have a steel shell on a foot ring with a weld seam, and a collar with handholds round a brass valve with a red handwheel and a yellow relief cap. They have a warning label, a red stencil, and rust, mud and scuffs. Everything printed is made up.
- `riverside_parts.propane( m, c, z0, size )` now only records a cylinder in `P.PROPANE`. A 100 lb one keeps the old 15 x 40 in collision hull in its zone model; the 20 lb pair never had collision. The pair in `rv` calls it with `size="20"`.
- Cylinders drawn inside a part (a trailer, the camper) are carried into the zone model's frame by `P.carry`, which `riverside_map.place_into` now calls. `write_scene` adds a top-level "Propane Cylinders" group after "Teddy", renderers only, each turned 137.5 degrees from the one before.
- Installed with `install_propane.py`. It runs the map's own trailers and pad code to get the placements, then cuts the old `rs_propane` and `rs_steel` faces from the live `riverside_pad.obj` and `riverside_terrace.obj`: 102 a 100 lb spot, 28 a 20 lb spot. Backups are in `output/asset-improvement-20261030/propane/before-install/`.
- `compare_propane.py` has to keep "Propane Cylinders" in its list of scene objects, or a look at the scene leaves them out.
- Seen in s&box after the zones recompiled (`check_installed.py`): the pad's row and the camper's pair. The porch one wasn't looked at. Not seen in a live match.
