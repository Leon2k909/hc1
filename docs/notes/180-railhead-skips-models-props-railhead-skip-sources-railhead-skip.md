## Railhead's skips (`models/props/railhead/skip`, `Sources/Railhead/skip/`, 2026-10-31)
- Replaced the two yard skips at the user's word: "we have a better skip than this on another map". The old one read as a sealed yellow box, because its rim box capped the whole top. The new one is Lowtide's welded open skip: `Tools/lowtide_skip.py`, built by `railhead_skips.write_model()` into a model of its own, sharing `materials/lowtide/lt_skip*`. Lowtide keeps its own.
- `skip_v2` (Boxyard's rebuild) and Boxyard's old green skip were the other candidates. Lowtide's kept Railhead's yellow, and it's the only other one that's open, welded and already on a map.
- `Tools/railhead_dress_detail.py`: `skip_bin` records a spot in `Tools/railhead_skips.py` and draws nothing, and `skip_bin_old` still builds the old box, for comparing. The scene's "Skips" group holds each with a renderer and a static `ModelCollider`.
- **Collision changed:** the old solid box is gone, so players can no longer stand on a lid. The model's own floor, wall hulls, rim and feet leave the inside open, as on Lowtide.
- **Moved 3 in** off the back wall (e 2158 to 2155), since the new rim is wider. The rim is 5.5 in from the wall, and the ends have over 15 in clear. It's 148 x 79 x 44 in against the old 136 x 69 x 47.
- Installed with `install_skips.py` and no map rerun: it cut 292 triangles a side, matching every corner, from `extinguisher-v2/east-base.obj` and `west-base.obj` (what the dress models build from) and from the generator's OBJs, and took the box out of the two live vmdls.
- **Compile race:** the editor began the west model's compile when its OBJ changed, a second before the vmdl was replaced, and missed the vmdl. Its fresh `_c` still had the old invisible box, and only the rays showed it. Touching the vmdl made the watcher recompile it, in about 7 s. When a tool writes an OBJ and a vmdl, check the collision with rays afterwards.
- Checked in a hidden copy of the map's scene, with rays and shots on both sides. **Not yet seen in a live match, and bots haven't been run around it.** Shots are in `output/asset-improvement-20261030/railhead-skip/`.
