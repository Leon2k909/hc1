## Spillway control desks v2 (`control_desk_v2`, the control room's four desks, 2026-10-31)
- `models/props/spillway/control_desk_v2/control_desk.vmdl` replaced the four `rv_desk` boxes and floating `schematic` screens in the upper control room, at the user's word after an in-engine before and after ("after is better so replace pls"). Sources, pipeline and faults found are in `Sources/Spillway/control-desk-v2/README.md`.

- Compiled and checked in place on 2026-10-31 (`capture.py compile`, in a hidden copy of the map): all four desks draw, the kneehole is closed, and rays hit the collision where expected. Not yet seen in a live match, and players haven't walked into it.
