# Round 1 report for the props area (both passes)

SUMMARY: Across both passes the props area has fixed every regression and all ten fixes the art director listed. The red EMU now reads as a train face from 15 to 30 m: red to the screen sill, wide red A-pillars, glossy black glass in rubber gaskets. Its bogies show through the cut-outs, with turned tyres, light grey dampers and a dust-toned bulkhead and recess, and its roof carries gutters, weld seams and cab antennas. The kiosks have lighter graphite cladding with shadow gaps, a brighter light box, a dressed roof (grey membrane, upstand, AC condenser, cable tray) and an optional third light. The snack machine has a lit name band and an aluminium door frame. The ticket machine has a bright, unbranded screen: the real "DB" mark and "BahnCard" are gone, and the trolley's "DB" mark is now the made-up "rhv". The buffer stops are desaturated with darker plates, and the Sh 2 boards have a grey back so they no longer show white from the concourse. The track has a brake-dust-dark crib band between the rails, and the track-head ballast stones sit on the lowered crib instead of floating. The magenta shed in the props renders came from the render stage, not the build: a new helper links the other areas' staged materials under my builds for renders. Last build (build10): 0 spot problems, 0 cut off, 0 Retakes pairs in sight. Nothing was written to Assets.
