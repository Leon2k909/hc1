This is the back of an LCD, not the screen and not a PC tower. Keep its overall screen width, height, orientation, desk position and approximate silhouette, with a convincing thin outer edge and a shallow rear electronics bulge. Show the new entire back and supporting stand from the exact same rear camera angle.
Scene and style: preserve the game's contemporary aquarium art direction, realistic restrained industrial design, dark metal and concrete, cool light from the surrounding tanks. Render as a plausible high-quality real-time game prop at the screenshot's camera and exposure, using readable surface values and soft specular highlights so the dark housing does not become an unlit black slab. Real buildable geometry, plausible ventilation and ports, small material details readable at this distance.
INVARIANTS: preserve the original environment, background architecture, floor, tabletop, camera, crop, perspective, first-person arm, weapon, green laser and white crosshair exactly. Existing foreground weapon and crosshair must still naturally overlap the replacement. The small background monitors can stay as they are. No new surrounding props.
Avoid: RGB, neon, emissive rear graphics, gamer styling, sci-fi armour, military hardware, hazard stripes, huge logos, large text, wildly exaggerated vents, exposed circuit boards, heavy rust, or excessive grime. No annotations or collage; one finished landscape screenshot.
Design direction A — restrained modern charcoal commercial LCD. Gently rounded rectangular graphite ABS shell, a softly curved shallow central volume with a precise fine perimeter seam. Two orderly shallow banks of fine horizontal ventilation slots near the upper rear, clearly cut into the shell. A central recessed square VESA interface covered by a neat small shroud, with a visibly engineered tilt joint into a slim charcoal pedestal. Low stable rounded rectangular base. A small muted technical label and four tiny flush screw recesses, no branding. Discreet downward-facing input recess beneath the bulge; two black cables clipped down the stand and entering the desk. Subtle fine plastic grain and very restrained edge wear. The shell reads graphite grey, with cool highlights separating its curved surfaces.
